Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum notice period for corporate orders?
Standard orders of 5 to 30 dozens require 48 hours notice. Custom branded logo toppers or acrylic towers require 3-4 business days. For rush same-day orders, please call our direct hotline: 9035477305.
Are eggless and vegetarian options available?
Yes! All our flagship Original Glazed® and assorted varieties are 100% vegetarian. We also provide certified eggless batches sealed separately with distinct allergy safety seals.
Can you deliver to multiple office buildings or remote employees?
Yes, our logistics fleet supports multi-drop corporate routes. Simply provide an address list and preferred morning delivery windows during the booking confirmation.
Do you provide corporate invoice billing (B2B Net 30)?
Registered corporate clients receive automated GST/Tax invoices, PO clearance, and Net 30 payment terms via our corporate accounts desk.
