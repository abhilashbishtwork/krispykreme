There's Something Special About
Krispy Kreme Doughnuts

Our Mission

To make the most awesome chocolate doughnuts and desserts in India, every single day.

Our Vision

To be the leader in sharing delicious desserts and creating joyful memories.

Our Belief

Chocolate isn't a flavour we add on. It's the whole point. Everything we make starts from there.

Fun. Fresh. Warm.

It started in 1937 in a small shop in Winston Salem, North Carolina, with one recipe for a doughnut so light it earned the name Original Glazed. Nearly nine decades on, that same hot off the line experience has grown into one of the world's most loved doughnut brands.

Krispy Kreme made its India debut in 2013 with its first store in Bengaluru, bringing that same freshness and warmth to Indian guests for the first time. Today, Krispy Kreme India is brought to you by Curefoods as the brand's master franchisee, running every store, kitchen and delivery under Krispy Kreme's global recipes and standards, with one important local twist. Our doughnuts here are made eggless, adapted for the tastes and preferences of Indian consumers, without losing what makes them unmistakably Krispy Kreme.

Doughnuts are where we started, and they're still what we do best. Chocolate is at the heart of it, and it's what we want India to think of first when chocolate desserts come to mind. But there's more on the menu too. Our coffee range, milkshakes and chillers are made to pair with every visit, whether that's a quick dessert fix, a coffee break with a friend, or a midnight chocolate craving. And if you're ever near Gurugram, drop by our Hot Light store at KLJ Square to watch the doughnuts come fresh off the line.
